Day 1 · Sat, Nov 28

Arrival & Lantau Island

  1. MNL → HKGClear immigration, get Octopus cards and eSIM or SIM.
  2. Ngong Ping Cable Car (Big Buddha)Scenic 25-minute ride from Tung Chung. Book tickets online ahead.
  3. Citygate OutletsRight beside the cable car station, great for lunch and bargains.
  4. Check in: Causeway Bay hotelMTR from Tung Chung.
  5. Avenue of StarsEvening harbor walk. Symphony of Lights show is at 8:00 PM.
Our suggestions: Store luggage in Tung Chung station lockers so no one hauls bags around the cable car area. Check cable car operating status close to the date, since maintenance closures happen. The Science Museum (Francis Pick) is on Day 2. If your flight lands early, you could swap it to Day 1, but it closes in the early evening, so it is tight. The optional night bus tour can go on Day 1 evening instead of Day 2 if everyone still has energy.
Day 2 · Sun, Nov 29

Peak, Harbor & Night Market

  1. Peak TramGo first thing in the morning to avoid long queues. Get Sky Pass tickets online.
  2. CentralMid-Levels Escalator, Man Mo Temple, lunch at a dai pai dong or tea restaurant.
  3. Star Ferry to Tsim Sha TsuiCheap, short, and one of the best harbor views.
  4. Hong Kong Science Museum (Francis Pick)Tsim Sha Tsui East, hands-on exhibits that suit the child. Check opening hours and its weekly closing day.
  5. Tsim Sha Tsui + SnoopyHarbour City and the waterfront. Confirm which Snoopy spot you want (Snoopy's World is in Sha Tin, farther away).
  6. Mong Kok & Temple Street Night MarketLadies' Market, street food and souvenirs. Best after 6 PM.
  7. Night bus tour (optional)Open-top night bus tour of the lit-up harbor and city. Book ahead for 15, and skip it if the senior and child are tired.
Our suggestions: This is the longest day. Let the senior and child rest at the hotel midafternoon and rejoin at Mong Kok by taxi. Hold wallets close and agree on a meeting point in crowded markets.
Day 3 · Mon, Nov 30

Hong Kong Disneyland

  1. Arrive at openingMTR Sunny Bay → Disneyland Resort Line (the Mickey-shaped windows are a hit with kids).
  2. Main attractionsPick 4 to 5 must-dos per person. Use the app for wait times.
  3. Evening show & fireworksReserve a dinner table ahead.
Our suggestions: Buy tickets in advance online. Bring the senior's own wheelchair, and consider a stroller rental for the child. Consider Premier Access for the top rides. Split into smaller groups with a fixed regroup time and spot.
Day 4 · Tue, Dec 1

Macau Day Trip

  1. Bus to MacauCross-border bus over the Hong Kong–Zhuhai–Macau Bridge, roughly 1 to 1.5 hours plus border checks. Bring passports and buy round-trip tickets.
  2. Ruins of St. Paul's & Senado SquareHeritage center, egg tarts and almond cookies.
  3. Fisherman's WharfThemed waterfront with replica landmarks, good for photos and a relaxed walk.
  4. The Londoner & The ParisianBig Ben–style facade, an Eiffel Tower replica, shops and restaurants on the Cotai Strip. The Venetian is just next door.
  5. Studio City (optional)Golden Reel Ferris wheel and Batman Dark Flight. Only if energy allows.
  6. Macau night bus tour (optional)See the lit-up Cotai Strip and old town after dark. Only if the last bus back to Hong Kong still works, otherwise skip it.
Our suggestions: Buses can be crowded, so go early and keep the group together at the border. Use the free resort shuttles on the Cotai Strip to save on taxis. Check the last bus back to Hong Kong. Wear comfy shoes, the old town is cobblestone.
Day 5 · Wed, Dec 2

Explore & Fly Home

  1. Explore Causeway BaySogo, Times Square, Lee Gardens, street food and last souvenirs near the hotel.
  2. AirportLeave about 4 hours before departure. Airport Express has in-town check-in at Hong Kong Station for some airlines.
Our suggestions: Keep this day light and plan around your flight time. Pack and check out first, and keep some HKD for last purchases.
Group planning

Smart tips for 15 people

Must-haves

  • Octopus card (everyone)
  • eSIM or physical SIM
  • Bonamine for motion sickness
  • Nik-nik spray

Getting around

  • Octopus card for everyone

Senior & child

  • Rest breaks every 2 to 3 hours
  • MTR priority seats, lifts at stations

Weather

  • Dry and mild, about 15 to 22°C
  • Light jacket for evenings and the Peak

Food

  • Share-style dishes work well
  • Cha chaan teng for cheap lunches
Before you go: Verify passport validity and visa-free rules for Hong Kong and Macau on official sites, and double-check opening hours, tickets and ferry schedules a week before departure.
